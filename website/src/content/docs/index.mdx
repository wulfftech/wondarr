Wondarr is a self-hosted \*arr whose unit is one song, not an album. You tell it which recordings you want: one at a time, a pasted list, a playlist or your existing music folder. It looks for the best copy on Soulseek first (through a bundled slskd), then YouTube Music, then torrents and usenet. It checks that the file really is that recording (duration and an AcoustID fingerprint), tags it, files it into your library, and keeps looking for a better copy until your quality cutoff is met. The library layouts are Plexamp-friendly: a Plexamp preset keeps albums tidy so Plex does not split or rename them.
